THE POST OFFICE

One last thing
before you leave.

There's a letter here. It was written by someone who knows a version of you that hasn't existed yet.

somewhere in the future

Dear Raysha,

I wonder what your room looks like now.

Maybe there are drawings everywhere. Maybe the desk is still a little messy. Maybe there are books stacked where books probably shouldn't be stacked.

Maybe you finally designed the places you used to imagine when you were younger.

I hope you are still curious. I hope you still notice beautiful things. I hope you still write when you have something worth saying.

And if life has not gone exactly according to plan, I hope you remember that a house does not become beautiful because every wall is perfect.

It becomes a home because someone stayed, cared, and kept building.

Keep building your life, one room at a time.

With hope,
someone from your past.

made with a little friendship, a lot of wishes, and a house that will always have the lights on.
