A LETTER for Raysha

Dear Raysha,

There are some friendships that don't need a grand explanation.

They simply happen. A conversation here, a laugh there, a thousand little moments that eventually become years.

Ours started in grade eight, and somehow, after everything that has happened since then, you're still here.

I don't know exactly where life will take us from here. Maybe our paths will become completely different. Maybe we'll be busy with lives we haven't imagined yet.

But wherever we end up, I hope there will always be a small part of this friendship that feels familiar.

I hope you keep reading. I hope you keep writing. I hope you keep dreaming about the things you want.

And when the time comes, I hope you become the architect you have always imagined yourself to be.

May you meet kind people. May your work make you proud. May your dreams find their way into the real world.

And when life gets confusing, I hope you remember that you don't have to know the entire blueprint yet. You only need to draw the next line.

with all the good wishes, always rooting for you.

and wherever we go from here...

I hope there will always be a little room for this.

since grade eight — and counting.
