A LETTER for Raysha

Dear Raysha,

I don't think every friendship needs a big explanation.

Sometimes, it is simply about having someone whose presence makes ordinary days feel a little more meaningful.

And I'm grateful for that.

More than anything, I hope you are kind to yourself while you're growing into everything you want to become.

I hope you meet people who appreciate your heart, your ideas, your weird little thoughts, your stories, and all the things that make you you.

I hope you keep reading books that change the way you see things. I hope you keep writing words that make someone stop and feel.

I hope your dream of becoming an architect takes you somewhere beautiful.

May you find courage when things feel uncertain. May you find patience when things take longer than expected. May you always have a reason to keep going.

And when you finally look back at everything you've built, I hope you feel proud of the person who built it.

always rooting for you, wherever life takes you.

for everything that's still ahead
