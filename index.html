R • 2040

somewhere in the future

Dear future Raysha,

So... did we make it?

Are you an architect now? Do you still carry a notebook everywhere? Do you still stop in the middle of a conversation because you suddenly thought of a sentence worth writing down?

I hope you do.

And if life didn't follow the blueprint, that's okay too. Some of the best things were never planned.

Keep reading. Keep writing. Keep becoming.

And when the days get difficult, please remember to talk to someone. Let people care about you. You were never meant to carry the whole world alone.

With all the good wishes I could fit into one little house,

someone who is very, very glad to know you.

before you go...

I hope wherever
you end up,
it feels like home.

I don't know what the future will look like. Maybe there will be buildings with your name somewhere in the plans. Maybe there will be books with your words between the pages. Maybe life will take you somewhere neither of us expected.

But wherever you go, I hope you know that you are loved, you are capable, and you never have to become someone else to deserve good things.

The light's always on.
You know where to find it.

— for Raysha, always.

made with a little navy, a few polka dots, and lots of good wishes.
