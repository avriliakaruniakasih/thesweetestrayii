somewhere in the future

Dear Raysha,

I hope you're somewhere you once dreamed of being.

Maybe you're standing inside a building you designed yourself, looking at the sunlight falling through a window you carefully imagined.

Maybe life has changed a hundred little things about you by then. And I hope that's okay. Growing was never supposed to mean staying exactly the same.

I hope you still read books that make you forget the time. I hope you still write things that make people stop for a moment.

And when things become difficult, please don't feel like you have to carry everything quietly.

Talk to your friends. Let someone sit beside you. Sometimes we don't need someone to fix everything. Sometimes we just need someone who stays.

I hope you become the architect you always wanted to be, but more than that, I hope you become someone who still has a soft place in her heart after everything life teaches her.

Build beautiful things, Raysha. And don't forget to build a beautiful life for yourself, too.

with a little faith in your future,

your friend, always.
