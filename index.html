FOR RAYSHA

there is a letter waiting for you

somewhere in the future

Dear Raysha,

I hope you still remember the girl who once had so many things she wanted to become.

Maybe one day you'll stand inside a building that you designed yourself. Maybe sunlight will fall through a window exactly the way you imagined it.

And when that day comes, I hope you pause for a moment and realize how far you've come.

Keep reading. Keep writing. Keep wondering. Keep making things.

You don't have to understand every part of the future today. Some things are meant to unfold slowly.

And whenever life feels too heavy, please remember that you don't have to carry every thought alone. Talk to someone. Sit beside a friend. Let the people who love you remind you that difficult days are still only days.

I hope the life you build is beautiful not only from the outside, but also from the inside.

May your drawings become buildings. May your words become stories. May your stories find people who need them.

And somewhere along the way, I hope you realize that you were already building something long before you ever touched a blueprint.

Take your time.
Keep going.
I'll always be cheering for you.

from someone who hopes you always find your way home.

the door is always open.

Until the next chapter.

Wherever life takes you, I hope you find a little piece of home wherever you go.
